Our philosophy

Thoughtful care, built around you.

Our approach begins with listening, a careful examination, and a conversation about your goals.

The body is a self-healing, self-regulating mechanism. At Harrison Chiropractic, the team’s philosophy is to support the body’s ability to function through chiropractic care.

The practice views the nervous system as central to communication throughout the body. Physical, chemical, or emotional stress may affect that communication. The team uses a thorough spinal examination to identify areas it may address through chiropractic care.

Every person’s history and needs are different. The team will explain its recommendations and discuss options with you.
