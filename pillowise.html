Sleep comfort

Pillowise custom-fit pillows.

A pillow recommendation based on your measurements, preferred sleeping position, and mattress feel.

01

About Pillowise

Pillowise was developed by Dutch physiotherapist Thijs van der Hilst. Its sizing approach uses a few measurements to help identify a pillow size for an individual.

02

How it works

The process records neck height and circumference, shoulder width, your usual sleeping position, and how soft or firm your mattress feels. Those details are entered into the Pillowise sizing calculator.

03

How it’s designed

The pillows use Tencel fabric and memory foam. The product information provided by Pillowise lists manufacturing in the Netherlands, a five-year warranty, and a 30-day return policy.
