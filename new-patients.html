01

What to expect

Your first visit typically lasts 30 to 40 minutes and includes a consultation, chiropractic and neurological examination, therapy, and an adjustment when appropriate after the examination.

We do not take X-rays in the office. Depending on your history and situation, outside X-rays may be requested.

02

Future visits

Follow-up visits typically last 15 to 30 minutes, depending on what is right for your visit.

After addressing your concerns, the team may share wellness-care suggestions. Choosing whether to continue is entirely up to you.

03

Insurance

The practice is out of network with insurance companies. Super-bills are available if you would like to contact your insurer about possible reimbursement.

Call the office if you have questions about rates or paperwork before scheduling.
